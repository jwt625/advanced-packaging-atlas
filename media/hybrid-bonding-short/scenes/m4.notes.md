# m4 - notes

"more to" ladder, shot m06: 2D transmission X-ray of C4 (controlled-collapse chip connection) joints under the
chip-on-wafer (CoW) assembly of a CoWoS-R+ class package, top view, greyscale. Single view (no `v` param). No text,
labels, arrows, scale bar or info bar. `meta.text = 'dark'`.

## What is drawn (top to bottom of the frame)

- Field under the CoW (y < 600): light grey 176 (interposer + dies in the beam), low-frequency brightness variation
  (+/- 3.5 %), faint routing shadows: orthogonal and 45 deg bands 8-22 px wide (darker and lighter, 3.5-7 % alpha) and
  four bundles of thin parallel traces, all clipped to y < 488.
- C4 joints on a square 150 um grid at 1 px/um: pitch 150 px, joint diameter 75 px, lighter core 35 px. Columns at
  x = 960 + k x 150 (one centred on x = 960, 13 visible). Rows centred at y = 462, 312, 162, 12; lowest joint bottoms
  at y = 499.5. Each joint: dark ring (about -130 grey levels) with a lighter core (about -58), soft edges, core
  slightly off-centre (+/- 1 px), size jitter 3 % (outer) / 10 % (core), thin bright edge-enhancement halo (+16).
- Interposer (CoW) edge on y = 600: grey step from 176 above to 214 below (less material in the beam), 2 px bright
  edge line (244) with a short bright falloff below it.
- Below the edge (substrate only): lighter field with seven large soft dark shadows (55-120 px radius, <= 10 % alpha),
  low contrast.
- Text band x 560-1360, y 500-592: plain light grey with one very faint 12 px routing shadow at y = 548 (2.2 % alpha).
- X-ray look: 1.6 px blur (focal spot / geometric unsharpness), then static detector grain (+/- 7 levels), once at
  init.
- Motion: horizontal pan 10 px/s leftward, held after t = 3 s (30 px total). The edge stays on y = 600.

## Deviations from reality

- The joint diameter comes from a different test vehicle (ectc51909.2023.00155-a-i3, typed `cu_pillar` in the atlas)
  because the CoWoS-R+ paper does not report it.
- Drawn as a complete square 150 um grid. In the CoWoS-R+ package the C4 pattern is re-arranged around 20 IPDs
  (integrated passive devices) on the interposer landside, which displace ~8 % of the C4 bumps (p.4); the real corner
  pattern is not resolved from the crops.
- Only one edge of the CoW is in frame (the corner itself lies off-frame); the crops' bright diagonal corner line is
  not drawn (its identity is not stated in either caption).
- The second, denser population of small dark dots seen in both crops is omitted: its identity was not found, and it
  would clutter a 0.125 s shot.
- The bright edge line and the halos around joints are drawn as edge enhancement (typical of inspection X-ray image
  processing, and seen as bright lines / halos in the crops); not a material.
- The large soft shadows below the edge stand for unspecified out-of-focus substrate features; positions chosen.

Draw time per frame (headless Chromium, SwiftShader, `APA.frame` + forced readback): 16-23 ms steady, 35 ms first
frame after load; init (sprites, blur, grain) about 0.8 s. Same frame for the same t regardless of call order (checked
with `toDataURL`). Sheet: `cache/video/sheets/m4__text-moreto.png` and `m4__text-moreto__full.png` (`--dur 0.2 --n 4`,
text "more to"; harness mean 315 ms/frame incl. screenshot).

## Styled text (overlay hook, 2026-10-23)

- `text` hook (loads `../lib/textfx.js`): the word as X-ray inspection overlay text burnt into the radiograph.
  Instrument mono 'APA Mono' (letter-spacing 0.02 em) at `info.size`, grey rgb(54, 54, 54) for tone 'dark' (scene
  default; rgb(232, 232, 232) for 'light', any other `text_color` as given), passed through the image's own
  processing: an edge-enhancement rim of opposite polarity just outside the strokes (12 offset copies at 3.4 px,
  16 % alpha, 2.2 px blur, strokes cut out; same idea as the joint halos), 1.6 px focal-spot blur, then sharper static
  detector grain +-7 levels keyed to display pixels. Centred on x 960, baseline 565; size grows with `text_size` /
  `text_grow`. No other text. Helper named `inkGrain` (the scene's own `grain` is used at init; a same-name helper
  overrode it in a first draft and blacked out the image).
- Flip-aware: overlay never mirrored; m06 is unflipped (band on the 176-grey field under the CoW). Also checked
  flip=1 with text_color dark (band on the lighter substrate field): legible.
- Contrast (measured, m06 params, 3 frames, overlay-alpha > 0.9 pixels vs the same pixels with the overlay hidden):
  median 4.73:1, per-pixel p5 4.21-4.29:1, min 3.89:1. Ink box (incl. rim) x 799-1117, y 507-571.
- Render: in-page render + hook + readback 66-68 ms steady (70 ms first); sheet harness mean 349, max 353 ms;
  `frames` m06 353 ms/frame (harness incl. screenshot).
- Sheet: `cache/video/sheets/m4__text_size-70.56__text_grow-27.81__text-moreto.png` and `..._full.png`
  (`--dur 0.15 --n 3`); shot frames `cache/video/frames/m06/`.
