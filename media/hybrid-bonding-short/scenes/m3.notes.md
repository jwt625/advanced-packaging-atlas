# m3 - notes

"more to" ladder, shot m05: BGA (ball grid array) on the package underside, macro photo, top view. Single view (no
`v` param). No text, silkscreen, A1 marker or scale bar. `meta.text = 'light'`.

## What is drawn (top to bottom of the frame)

- Package underside: dark teal solder resist (SR, rgb 26 / 82 / 84) with a broad overhead sheen (brighter upper
  left), faint glossy reflections at the interstitial sites above y = 470, mild vignette.
- Reflowed SAC305 balls on a square 1.0 mm grid at 140 px/mm: pitch 140 px, ball diameter 84 px (600 um). Columns at
  x = 960 + k x 140 (one centred on x = 960, 14 visible). Rows centred at y = 458, 318, 178, 38 (and a partial row at
  -102); lowest ball bottoms at y = 500, so no ball enters the text band.
- Ball shading (per pixel, per ball sprite, built once): grey body lit from the upper left (Lambert term), limb tinted
  toward the teal surroundings and darkened to a thin outline; broad flat-topped specular core (radius 0.52 R) with a
  small sharp glint; mottling = 7-12 soft darker blobs per ball (dimpled reflowed surface) plus, on about 22 % of
  balls, 2-5 distinct dark spots; static fine grain; highlight position and size jitter +/- 2 %.
- Soft contact shadow (to R + 5 px, offset down-right) under every ball.
- Substrate edge on y = 600: catch light on the SR in y 593-600, then a 9 px laminate side band (olive, darkening)
  below the line, then a near-black backdrop to the bottom of the frame.
- Text band x 560-1360, y 500-592: plain SR, no array features.
- Photo softness: 0.7 px Gaussian blur over the whole image (once, at init).
- Motion: horizontal pan 10 px/s leftward, held after t = 3 s (30 px total; 1.25 px over a 0.125 s shot). The edge
  stays on y = 600.

## Deviations from reality

- Balls drawn at the 600 um nominal sphere diameter in plan view; the plan-view diameter of a ball reflowed onto a
  500 um SR opening (before board mount) is not reported.
- The 9 px laminate band below the edge implies a very slight tilt (a strict top view shows no side wall); kept so the
  edge reads as a physical edge on y = 600.
- No A1 corner marker, silkscreen, fiducials or scale bar (text-like / not needed).
- Mottling and dark spots are a look, not a measured surface; per-ball variation is seeded and static.

## Render

Draw time per frame (headless Chromium, SwiftShader, `APA.frame` + forced readback): 20-24 ms steady, 102 ms first
frame after load; init (sprites + blur) about 0.9 s. Same frame for the same t regardless of call order (checked with
`toDataURL`). Sheet: `cache/video/sheets/m3__text-moreto.png` and `m3__text-moreto__full.png` (`--dur 0.2 --n 4`,
text "more to"; harness mean 211 ms/frame incl. screenshot).

## Styled text (overlay hook, 2026-10-23)

- `text` hook (loads `../lib/textfx.js`): the word as screen-printed legend ink (solder-resist silkscreen). Monoline
  strokes with round ends: 'APA Grotesk' 300 (letter-spacing 0.06 em) at `info.size`, filled plus stroked (round
  join / cap, width 0.05 em); edge ragged by the screen mesh (1.3 px blur, then alpha thresholded at 0.5 against
  static seeded noise at 2 px and 8 px scales, keyed to display pixels); faint mesh weave (+-5 %) in the ink; matte
  off-white rgb(240, 238, 226) for tone 'light' (rgb(18, 22, 22) for 'dark', any other `text_color` as given); then
  the macro photo's 0.7 px lens blur. Centred on x 960, baseline 565; size grows with `text_size` / `text_grow`.
  No other text.
- Flip-aware: overlay never mirrored; on m05 (flip=1, text_color light) the band shows the black backdrop below the
  laminate edge. Also checked unflipped (band on teal SR, where it reads as a real legend): legible.
- Contrast (measured, m05 params, 3 frames, same method as m1): median 16.26-16.27:1, per-pixel p5 14.68:1,
  min 11.81:1. Ink box x 835-1082, y 517-567.
- Render: in-page render + hook + readback 25-27 ms steady (118 ms first); sheet harness mean 325, max 430 ms;
  `frames` m05 271 ms/frame (harness incl. screenshot).
- Sheet: `cache/video/sheets/m3__flip-1__text_color-light__text_size-67.37__text_grow-26.55__text-moreto.png` and
  `..._full.png` (`--dur 0.15 --n 3`); shot frames `cache/video/frames/m05/`.
