# m7: two dielets on a Si-IF, fine-pitch links across the gap (optical brightfield, top view)

Shot m09 in the "more to" ladder (`refs_more_to.md` section m7). Single view, no `v` param. Overlay tone:
`meta.text = 'dark'` (dielet backside, text band luminance about 0.73; checked with `text="more to"`).

Not a hybrid bond: the dielets are joined to the Si-IF by direct Cu-Cu thermocompression bonding (TCB) with in-situ
formic acid vapor (Cu pad on the dielet to Cu pillar on the Si-IF), no dielectric bond. It is the chiplet-to-chiplet
rung of the ladder.

Cu pillars (4 um, 9.8 um pitch) sit under the dielets at both ends of each 350 um link, so they are hidden in this
view. The wires continue under both dielets (350 um link vs 59 um gap).

## Choices (not found in the atlas; stated basis)

- Scale 4 px/um (chosen, per refs_more_to.md).
- Wire width 2 um (half pitch). Not found.
- Bundle width 256 um = 64 x 4 um, one bundle centred on x = 960. This reads "4 um pitch" as the pitch of all 64
  wires in one plane. With two metal layers the in-plane pitch per layer could differ; the paper does not say.
- Spacing between neighbouring interfaces along the edge: not found. No second bundle is drawn; the gap outside the
  bundle is plain dark floor.
- Gap floor is dark and defocused: brightfield focused on the dielet backsides, the Si-IF surface lies one dielet
  thickness below (thickness not found) and is shadowed by the sidewalls (look of fig ectc51906.2022.00332-f01 (c)).
- Colours: dielet backside beige-grey, wires gold-tan, warm microscope cast, vignette (corners 16-28 % darker, warmer).
  Residue stain and particle are static, low-contrast details, kept out of the text band x 560-1360, y 500-592.
- Motion: horizontal stage drift 10 px/s (30 px at t = 3 s, clamped beyond). No vertical motion.

## Deviations

- Motif: the array is below the edge (in the gap), not above it. The edge on y = 600 is the upper dielet's edge, the
  object that carries the bonded pillars.
- Composition is constructed (one interface bundle centred in a 480 um wide field); interface positions along the
  dielet edge are not reported.
