# m2 - notes

"more to" ladder, shot m03 (3 frames, 0.125 s): top-down photo of the 50 x 50 mm embedded-glass-interposer package
(Georgia Tech GPE test vehicle 2): 2 SoC emulators + 8 HBM emulators. Single view, no `v` param. No silkscreen, logo,
labels or scale bar. `meta.text = 'light'`.

## What is drawn (orthographic top view, 27 px/mm)

- Package x 285-1635 (50 mm), bottom edge on y = 600, top off-frame (y = -750). Thin lighter laminate band (2-3 px) on
  the visible edges; last package row y 599, first backdrop row y 600 (checked).
- Teal solder resist with a static blurred orange-peel mottling.
- Die field: lighter grey-green opening around the dies with a 0.5 mm dot grid of exposed pads; it ends 1 mm below
  the lowest HBMs and, under the SoC, at the SoC fillet (y 493).

  the 1.1 mm SoC gap are off-frame above.
- HBM: 5.5 x 7.7 mm portrait (148.5 x 208 px), inner edge 3.5 mm from the SoC, pairs centred on SoC-2 with a 1.5 mm
  gap: lower HBMs y 230-438, upper HBMs y -18-190; left x 460.5-609, right x 1311-1459.5. All four HBMs of SoC-2 in
  frame.
- Between each HBM pair and the SoC: brown routing over the embedded die (7.2 mm tall, centred on the pair gap,
  3.5 mm wide where visible), fine fanned traces, short teal bars at its ends.
- CUF fillets: translucent amber, 0.5 mm, around every die.
- Die backsides: near-mirror Si, diagonal specular gradient (light upper left), faint grinding arcs, 2 px bevel
  highlight top/left and shade bottom/right; HBMs a touch lighter than the SoC.
- Edge pad columns: 5 columns per side at 0.8 mm spacing, 1.5-4.7 mm from the left/right edges, pads 7 px at 0.55 mm
  vertical pitch, 1.4-23 mm above the bottom edge (x < 412 and x > 1508: outside the text band).
- Backdrop: dark neutral radial gradient, soft package shadow.
- Photo look: 1 px blur everywhere except the 6 px strip at y = 600, broad sheen, mild vignette, static grain
  (+-5 levels), seeded once (seed 2050).
- Motion: horizontal pan 10 px/s leftward, held after 3 s (30 px); the prebuilt image is 46 px wider than the frame.
- Text band x 560-1360, y 500-592: plain teal below SoC-2 (fillet ends at y 493). Sampled band luminance 0.116-0.130
  (below the 0.35 limit), contrast with the light overlay 5.2-5.6:1.

## Deviations

- Scale 27 px/mm instead of the refs composition's 20 px/mm, and SoC-2 bottom 4.45 mm above the edge instead of the
  refs' ~7.6 mm. The 7.6 mm value comes from the f09 floorplan, which is not to scale (its 19 mm SoC measures ~17 mm by eye against the 50 mm outline);
  2 x 20 mm SoCs + 1.1 mm gap on 50 mm leave only 8.9 mm, so 7.6 mm at the bottom would leave 1.3 mm at the top.
  At 20 px/mm the centred SoC edge would fall at y 511, inside the text band; 27 px/mm puts it at y 480 (fillet to
  493). Consequence: SoC-1 is off-frame (no sliver above SoC-2); the frame shows one SoC and its four HBMs.
- HBM pair gap 1.5 mm and the pair centring are crop estimates; the f09 floorplan and the f06(b) photo disagree
  slightly.
- Edge pad columns compressed to 1.5-4.7 mm from the edges (crop of f06(a) suggests ~2.5-7 mm) so they stay outside
  x 560-1360 at this scale; pad count / pitch are look, not data.
- Die-field outline simplified to one stepped polygon; brown routing pattern is schematic (fanned lines), not the real
  layout.
- Silkscreen text ("Embedded glass interposer package", logo, author / date) omitted (contract).
