## Choices (not found in the atlas; stated basis)

- Scale 30 px/um (chosen, per refs_more_to.md); 1.8 um optical width rather than the 2 um target, since this is an
  optical image.
- Line length and termination pattern: not found. Lines end in one row (as in fig ectc51909.2023.00298-f07) at
  y = 500, 3.3 um (100 px) above the plane edge (chosen, keeps the text band clear).
- Colours: Cu orange-salmon on near-black dielectric (look of f07); vignette (corners 19-25 % darker).
- Motion: horizontal stage drift 10 px/s (30 px at t = 3 s, clamped beyond). No vertical motion.

## Deviations

- **Constructed:** the wide Cu plane / bus below y = 600 and its edge on y = 600. The reference shows the line ends
  over bare dielectric and no plane; the plane gives the scene its y = 600 edge (motif) and the 3.3 um gap is chosen.
- Grain texture, specks and edge wobble are seeded procedural texture, not measured.
