## Structure and composition (top to bottom on screen)

- **Processor chiplet top surface** (die passivation field, neutral mid-dark grey) with the interface-region Cu stud
  array.
  - The studs are flat-topped cylinders, seen at a 30 deg tilt.
  - Top ellipse: mid-light, with a rough plated texture and a bright rim. The far (vacuum-side) rim is brightest.
  - Front sidewall: brighter toward the silhouettes (grazing incidence), with a dark corner at the base.
  - A soft contact shadow sits around each base, offset to the right. The detector is assumed upper left, and
    surfaces facing it are brighter.
- **Array position:**
  - Lowest row: stud base fronts at y = 500.
  - Top-ellipse centres at y = 418.4, 210.6, 2.8, and so on.
  - Columns at 960 + k 160 px.
- **y = 600:** the die edge, the strongest SE edge in the frame (bright 3-5 px edge effect).
  - Below it is the die sidewall face, seen at the 30 deg tilt. It is dim (shadowed from the detector), with a faint
    vertical texture.
- **Text band** x 560-1360, y 500-592: plain field speckle. No studs, no scan-line gain rows.
- **Static SEM vocabulary:**
  - fine field speckle and a slow brightness drift (charging / gain);
  - per-stud +/- 1 % size and sub-pixel scatter;
  - about 1 px probe blur;
  - screen-fixed static detector noise and 12 scan-line gain rows, kept out of y 490-604.

## Deliberate deviations

- **Array extent:** the dense 20 x 30 um stud array fills the full frame width and every visible row.
  - In the reference die, the dense field is a strip along the interface edge, next to coarser studs and an L-shaped
    corner mark.
  - The strip's row count and length are not stated. The frame shows about 87 um of depth (3 rows plus a partial row).
- **Omitted:**
  - the optional 60 um / 180 um-pitch coarse stud (480 px wide at this scale; it would crowd the frame);
  - the L-shaped corner mark;
  - the SEM info bar and scale bar.
- **Die sidewall face:** drawn dim (detector shadowing, my choice) so that the edge line stays the dominant feature.
  - Pure SE yield geometry would make a face at 60 deg local incidence brighter.
  - The sidewall texture direction is my choice, per `refs_more_to.md` ("faint vertical texture").
- **Brightness balance:** sidewall brightness and top-surface roughness are stylized from SE contrast rules and the
  crop look. They are not measured.
- **Edge distance:** `refs_more_to.md` says "~10 um after foreshortening". The stated composition (bases at 500,
  edge at 600) gives 14.4 um on the die surface. I kept the composition.
