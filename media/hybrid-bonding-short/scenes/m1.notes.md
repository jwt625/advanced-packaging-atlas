## What is drawn (top to bottom, orthographic top view, 2.4 px/mm)

- Panel x 348-1572 (510 mm), bottom edge on y = 600, top off-frame (y = -636; 515 mm).
- Dielectric surface (solder resist over build-up): dark green.
- 6 x 6 units, centred on the panel, 72 mm pitch in each half, plus a 12 mm wider centre street in both axes (unit
  centres at +-42, +-114, +-186 mm from the panel centre). Each unit: a light-gold pad field 33 x 47 mm (79 x 113 px,
  portrait) with fine horizontal striations (4.6 px), inside a darker SR frame (7 mm margin left/right, 6 mm
  top/bottom). Thin lighter saw-street lines on the cell boundaries and a faintly lighter centre street.
- Visible: 3 full unit rows (pad-field bottoms at y 485, 312, 139) and the 6 columns; the horizontal centre street
  is at the frame top.
- A few small bright marks on the vertical centre street (y < 460) and on the right rim.
- Rim: pale edge band 3.3 mm (8 px, y 590-598 at the bottom edge), a 2 px dark-brown outline (the exposed panel
  side, y 598-600), then the table. Last panel row y 599, first table row y 600 (checked on the full frame).
- Table: blue-grey gradient, extra 4 px blur (out of the panel plane), soft contact shadow below / right.
- Photo look: whole image blurred 1.2 px except the 6 px strip around y = 600 (edge kept crisp), broad upper-left
  sheen, slight cool-to-warm shift across the panel, low-frequency mottling, mild vignette, static grain (+-5 levels),
  all seeded once at init (seed 1510).
- Motion: zoom 1.00 -> 1.02 about (960, 600), ease-out over 3 s, held after; the edge stays on y = 600.
- Text band x 560-1360, y 500-592: plain dark green (SR frames end at y 499). Sampled band luminance 0.057-0.087,
  contrast with the light overlay 6.8-8.7:1.

## Deviations

- Pad-field bottom 48 mm above the edge (y 485) instead of the refs composition's ~70 mm (y 432): 70 mm is not
  consistent with a centred 6 x 6 grid at 72 mm pitch with 47 mm fields on 515 mm (it would leave 38 mm at the top);
  the measured brightness profile of f06 gives ~50-54 mm at the bottom. Effect: 3 full unit rows in frame instead of
  ~2.5; the band stays plain green.
- SR frame margins (7 / 6 mm) and the 1.4 px street lines are chosen from the crop look, not measured.
- Rim drawn 3.3 mm (refs composition) although the crop suggests ~6-8 mm, so the bright band stays below the glyphs.
- Orthographic top view; the reference photo is slightly oblique (keystone), corrected so the edge is horizontal on
  y = 600.
- Silkscreen, fiducial labels and any text on the panel omitted (contract).

## Styled text (overlay hook, 2026-10-23)

  (letter-spacing 0.05 em) at `info.size`, resampled into a dot matrix of overlapping ablation spots (cell = size / 12,
  spot radius 0.58 cell, grid anchored at the pen origin, per-spot brightness 0.93-1.0 seeded), frosted pale ink
  rgb(234, 228, 212) for tone 'light' (rgb(26, 34, 30) for 'dark', any other `text_color` used as given). Then the
  photo's look on the ink only: 1.2 px blur, the panel's upper-left sheen (radial from (760, 120)), static grain +-5
  levels keyed to display pixels. Centred on x 960, baseline `info.baseline` (565); size grows with `text_size` /
  `text_grow`. No other text.
- Flip-aware: the overlay is never mirrored; on m01 (flip=1, text_color light) the band shows the blurred blue-grey
