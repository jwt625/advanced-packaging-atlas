# m5: "more to" m07, reflected-light optical top view of an etched through-glass via (TGV) array

Single view (no `v` param). The overlay tone is `meta.text = 'dark'`. The text band luminance is 0.63 mean (std 0.02, 5th-95th
percentile 0.59-0.66), measured on the full-res frame. There is no text in the scene.

## Structure and composition (top to bottom on screen)

- Laser-modified borosilicate glass coupon, top surface, after etch, before metallization. Shown as a flat
  blue-grey / lavender field with a square array of open through-holes.
  - Holes are dark slate: the light leaves through the hole, and the stage under it is dark.
  - Each hole has a crisp top edge, a thin dark contact line at the edge, and a lighter ring 3-7 px inside the edge
    (the tapered wall).
  - A very faint ring sits about 14 px inside (an out-of-focus waist of the hourglass via).
- Lowest row centred at y = 425.8 (hole bottoms at y = 500). Rows above at 200.8 and -24.2 (clipped).
- Columns centred at 960 + k 225 px.
- **y = 600:** the diced coupon edge. Above it: a 1-2 px bright chamfer highlight with a faint darkening 4 px inside.
  Below it: the dark stage, with a short scattered glow under the edge.
- Text band x 560-1360, y 500-592: plain glass field. No holes, no particles.
- Static microscope vocabulary:
  - slow illumination non-uniformity, a 10 % vignette and faint surface haze;
  - 9 small dark surface particles, kept out of the band;
  - hole size scatter of +/- 1 % and +/- 0.4 px position scatter;
  - screen-fixed static sensor noise.

## Deliberate deviations

- **Coupon edge:** the array edge distance and the coupon edge are not in the source, so they are my choice.
  - `refs_more_to.md` says "58 um from the last hole edge", but it also puts the hole bottoms at 500 and the edge at 600.
  - Those two placements give 33.3 um. The 58 um is the distance from the hole centre.
  - I kept the stated composition (bottoms at 500, edge at 600).
- **Colours:** stylized from the microscope look (blue-grey field, slate holes).
  - Real colour depends on the illumination and camera white balance.
  - Microscope label overlays (measurement circles, crosshairs, dimension text) are omitted.
- **Waist ring:** speculative and kept very faint. Whether the waist shows in reflected light depends on focus and NA.
