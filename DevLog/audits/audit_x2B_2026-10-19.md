# audit_x2B: fresh-context audit of the advanced-packaging extraction DB, group B (2026-10-19)

Snapshot: data/*.csv as merged on 2026-10-19 (209 papers, 673 TVs, 1233 figures rows). Read-only; no project file
edited except this report. Method:
- For each sampled paper: read text.md in full and dumped every row from every data/*.csv table plus
  data/evidence/<id>.yaml.
- Compared values against text, tables and page renders, with 300-400 dpi re-crops where labels were small.
- Re-rendered every figures row with `scripts/crop_figures.py --spec PAGE:BBOX` into scratch and opened each crop.
- Ran DB-wide checks by script over all data/*.csv. `scripts/validate_db.py` on data/: 0 errors, 0 warnings.
- Helper scripts (scratch only): dump.py, cropall.sh, sheet.py, dbcheck.py.

## 3. Ranked defects (row ids, evidence, fix)

1. ectc51687.2025.00061-a-i1: speculative interconnect typing (level die_to_bridge, ic_type microbump, metallurgy solder).
   - p.3 Fig. 5 cross-section labels only "EMIB pitch" bumps under the S10 FPGA and the EIC. Sec. IV.B (p.4) names solder bumps only for the EIC-PIC die-to-die bond.
   - Sec. IV.B also says "Inline TCB FAR bonding process was used for all die bonding to substrate". This implies solder but names no bump type.
   - Fix: ic_type unspecified. Keep metallurgy solder with a note "inferred from FAR" (or clear it). Notes already say the pitch value is not given.
2. eptc59621.2023.10457909-c: technology_name = M-series on the real-panel TV.
   - The TV note says "Inferred M-series: Fig. 10 Simul. values equal the M-series values of Fig. 8".
   - The paper never names the real sample's structure (p.4 Sec. 4, p.4 Fig. 10). The inference itself is reasonable, since the Simul. curve -1.37/-2.81/-7.62 matches p.3 Fig. 8.
   - Rule (l) puts inferred mappings in notes only. Fix: clear technology_name and keep the note.
3. ectc51846.2026.00365-h: package_x_mm 85, package_y_mm 110 and reticle_multiple 5 sit under row basis `stated` with no override.
   - The schema defines `stated` as a non-numeric fact. The evidence entry for the same cell says design_target.
   - Same pattern for reticle_multiple on -i (>7X), -j (2X) and -k (4X); -j/-k override only the source.
   - Fix: row basis design_target, or an override "package_x_mm:design_target;...".
   - Validator rule: numeric TV/component columns under basis stated -> warn.
4. ectc51909.2023.00154-a-c1, -c2, -c3, -c4: gap_um = 60 on all four die component rows.
   - The paper gives one statement: "The gap of the each chip was 60um" (p.2 Sec. II).
   - This is the same copy class as audit_x1 item 4 (00106 gap in c1 and m7).
   - Fix: keep it once, on the interposer-level row or as a TV geometry metric ("die-to-die gap, all dies", 60 um), and clear the copies.
5. ectc51846.2026.00092-a-s02, -a-s06, -a-s07: the positions of these steps are not stated in the paper. The rows' own notes say so: "Order relative to CoW not explicit", "Position after oS inferred", "Attach order not stated".
   - p.2 Fig. 3 gives only CoW -> micropillar loop -> oS. Carrier coating (s02), sealant dispense (s06) and lid attach (s07) are described without order (p.3 Sec. I.B, p.5 Sec. IV.A).
   - Rule 7 says "the ordered flow as the paper describes it". The notes are honest, but seq still asserts an order.
   - Fix: a `seq_basis` (stated | inferred) column or a `performed`-like flag, or put unordered steps under a separate flow_name with seq restarting.
6. ectc51846.2026.00092-f01 (Fig. 1, p.2): the `shows` text names a sealant, HBM, an organic RDL interposer, substrate and BGA.
   - The drawing has no labels, and the caption says only "Schematic of direct-liquid-cooling on 3.3X CoWoS-R TV". The names come from the body text.
   - SKILL.md step 5: nothing that is not in the figure or its caption. Fix: "Unlabelled cross-section schematic of manifold lid with inlet/outlet over micropillared dies on interposer and substrate".
7. ectc51687.2025.00061-a-m1: variant "after pre-screen thermal bake (original TCB profile)".
   - The pre-screen bake comes before TCB (p.5 Sec. IV.C), so no TCB profile applies to the ~50% figure.
   - Fix: variant "incoming substrates after pre-screen bake at solder melting temperature".
8. ectc51687.2025.00009-f04 (Fig. 9, p.4, manual bbox 0.14,0.64,0.42,0.825): y1 cuts through the "(a)" and "(b)" panel labels.
   - The caption sits just below. Fix: y1 about 0.835.

Convention items (not counted as errors):
- has_process_flow calibration. Both papers below fit the literal rule ("discrete process steps"), but neither is an
  ordered fabrication flow. The DB has 201 yes vs 8 no.
  - eptc59621.2023.10457909 = yes on two "cure" steps (-c-s01, -c-s02). They are the warpage read-out stages of p.4 Fig. 10; no conditions are given and the paper is a simulation study.
  - ectc51846.2026.00365 = yes on generic dispense/cure/CSAM rows with no conditions (-d-s01, -e-s01..s03, -f-s01, -g-s01..s03).
- Baseline repeated within one TV: eptc59621.2023.10457909-a-m1, -a-m4, -a-m9 and -a-m21 are one model run (-7.62 mm).
  - It appears as the baseline of three sweeps and as the Passivation 2 stage.
  - Acceptable for per-sweep plotting, but aggregations count it four times. Mark baselines in variant ("1.0X (baseline)").
- ectc51846.2026.00365-g-m1: statistic max plus qualifier value:le for "up to 3.4 mm". The le is redundant with max:
  the d6 = 3.4 mm label in p.4 Fig. 6(a) is the measured maximum.

## 6. Recommendations (short)

1. Validator warnings for:
   - (a) numeric TV/component cells under basis `stated` without an override (defect 3);
   - (b) identical (parameter, value, value_text, unit, variant, locator) on sibling TVs;
   - (c) identical gap_um/count copied across components of one TV.
2. process_steps: add `seq_basis` (stated | inferred) so inferred ordering is visible (defect 5). Make has_process_flow
   three-valued (flow | steps_only | no), or require >= 3 steps with at least one condition or material for `yes`.
   The present 201/8 split does not separate fabrication-flow papers from results papers that mention a cure.
3. Figures:
   - The 93% at exactly 6 figures suggests agents fill to the cap. Instruct "2-6, only figures that add a distinct view
     or data source".
   - Add a rule that `shows` may name only labelled items when a drawing has no labels.
4. Apply the audit_x1 fixes still open (section 5) before the next merge. Add value_min/value_max (and n_fail/n_total)
   to metrics to absorb the ~180 range-like value_text rows.
5. Fix defects 1-8 in place (8 rows plus 4 basis cells). None changes a stored number.
