## 3. Ranked defects (row ids, evidence, fix)

1. Sibling-TV copies of one paper-level statement: 00265 adhesion rows.
   - a-m21, b-m9, c-m5 and d-m13 are four identical "well attached after TC" rows (pass).
   - a-m22, b-m10, c-m6 and d-m14 are four identical "stiffener separated in an early cycle count" rows (fail).
   - p.5 Sec. IV.E says "The stiffener of the reference package with 70-um-thick adhesive separated ... The ratio of the
     delaminated sample was higher on the substrates with lower CTE core."
   - No per-core result is given. a-m22 (core A, the highest CTE) asserts a fail that the paper does not state for core A.
   - counts.py confirms these are the only two identical-claim groups in the sample. The validator warning recommended in
     audit_x1 section 4 is not yet catching them.
   - Fix: one row each on a pooled TV (or `applies_to`). Put "delaminated ratio higher on lower-CTE cores" in value_text
     and leave result empty for the per-core reading.
2. tcpmt.2021.3104326-b-i1 resistance_mohm = 47.8 (copied from a-i1).
   - 47.8 mOhm is the SnAg Kelvin value at 300 K (p.6 Table I; p.4 Sec. III).
   - p.3 Fig. 3 caption: "These data have been obtained on assemblies without underfill."
   - b-i1 is the underfill build, and its own notes concede "not separated by underfill".
   - Fix: clear b-i1.resistance_mohm. Keep b-m2 ("similar to assemblies without underfill", stated).
3. 00026-a-m9 (95% predicted yield), -a-m10 (58% utilization), -a-m11 (9 gross dies) hang off TV a.
   - Their source is p.2 Fig. 2, a generic wafer-utilization model vs interposer size (x = 1.4 to 8).
   - The paragraph ends "Figure 3. [9-11]", and the curve is not a property of the built TV.
   - The same evidence file excludes "CoW join yield up to 99.5% ... not this TV" and the Fig. 1 roadmap as generic, so
     the exclusions are inconsistent.
   - Fix: move all three to `excluded`, or to a paper-level/design_concept TV with basis author_estimate.
4. 00026-a-m1: value 1000 um, `le`, basis measured, statistic max, parameter "wafer warpage".
   - p.3 Sec. III.A: "wafer warpage could be well controlled within 1000um spec".
   - 1000 is the spec limit, not a measured value.
   - Fix: value empty; value_text "within 1000 um spec, symmetric dome"; pass_criterion "<= 1000 um"; result pass.
5. 00183-a-i1, -a-i2, -a-i3: alignment_um 0.1 with qualifier `alignment_um:le`.
   - p.3 Sec. III.B: "bonding misalignment was 100 nm or less in 93% of cases". The `le` bound applies to 93% only.
   - The value is also a pooled W2W measurement copied to all three pitch rows (p.3 Fig. 7).
   - a-m5 (93%) already captures it correctly.
   - Fix: clear alignment_um on i1-i3 (or keep it with `approx` and a note "P93"). Add an alignment statistic field
     (section 5).
6. tcpmt locators: the 260 C stacking-reflow statement is on p.5 ("reflow at 260 C at the end of the stacking process",
   after the `<!-- page 5 -->` marker), not p.4.
   - Affected: a-i1 and b-i1 (locator "p.4 Sec. IV"), a-s05 and b-s05 (notes "p.4"), and the evidence conflicts entry.
   - Fix: p.5 Sec. IV.
7. 00026-a-s02 "IPD bonding" at seq 2.
   - Its own note says "Order from abstract listing". The abstract lists "fine pitch ubump joining, flux clean, IPD bonding
     and underfill gap filling" (p.1).
   - The paper never gives an order, and the listed CoW flux clean was dropped while the IPD position was kept.
   - Fix: keep the step, but set seq by the text's explicit order only. Or add the CoW flux clean between s01 and s02 and

8. 00110-e-s01..s04 are the proposed Fig. 6 trench-imprint/squeegee flow (p.3 Sec. III.A "is proposed"; p.4 "is expected

## 5. Recommendations (short)

1. Validator: enforce the sibling-duplicate warning from audit_x1 section 4 (identical parameter/value_text/variant/locator
   across TVs of one paper). It would have caught defect 1.
2. Validator: warn when rank 1's kind is below another row's kind in the order
   cross_section > stack_schematic > package_photo > other.
3. SKILL.md: values read from generic model or roadmap figures (utilization, yield-vs-size, cost curves) are paper-level
   context. They go in `excluded`, not on a TV.
4. SKILL.md: specs and thresholds ("within X um spec") go in pass_criterion, never in value.
5. Schema: interconnects.alignment_stat (max | p93 | mean ...), or keep alignment only in metrics. n_samples means
   physical samples; data-point counts go in condition.
6. Schema/SKILL: process_steps.performed = no for proposed or concept flows; pair it with tv_kind design_concept
   (audit_x1 section 5 item 2).
7. Shared flows: a flow_id/applies_to mechanism (audit_x1 section 5 item 6) would remove ~150 copy rows from 00265 alone.
8. Locator self-check script: for each numeric cell, grep the number on the cited text.md page and flag misses. It would
   have caught defect 6.
