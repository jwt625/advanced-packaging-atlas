# DevLog-015: hybrid bonding short video

Request (Wentao, 2026-10-22): a short release-style video for advanced packaging, modeled on the Claude Opus 5.5 /
Haiku 5.5 / Kimi launch videos. Figure crops from the atlas serve only as visual references; every scene is generated
by code (SVG / Canvas / JS). Components and topologies as accurate as possible; dimensions to scale where critical
(pitch, bump / ball size). Subagents find references and build scenes; fresh-context audit; then assembly.

## Decisions (Wentao, 2026-10-22)

1. Technology: hybrid bonding, told as one interface shrinking in pitch, BGA to sub-micron Cu-Cu hybrid bond.
2. Audio: Wentao supplies a track later. Until then the MP4 carries a silent AAC track. Frames and timings will be
   adjusted to the track's tempo (Wentao, 2026-10-22): every shot carries a `beats` count in `edit.json`;
   `render_video.py retime --bpm B --offset S` rewrites the frame counts so cuts land on the beat grid (cumulative
   rounding), `assemble --audio-start S` picks the entry point in the track. Scenes must stay valid for t in [0, 3] s.
3. Format: Opus 5.5 video format. Reference file (downloaded 2026-10-22): 1920x1080, 25 fps, H.264 High yuv420p
   ~5.1 Mb/s, AAC-LC 48 kHz stereo 128 kb/s, 20.05 s. Structure: open on a glowing horizon, a montage of texture shots
   that share one geometric motif at a fixed screen position, cuts accelerating from ~0.5 s to ~0.25 s, a few words
   riding the motif in the last third, product-name card, logo card, loop back to the opening frame.
4. Location: `media/hybrid-bonding-short/` in this repo. Rendered frames stay in `cache/video/` (git-ignored).

## Concept

Motif: the bond interface is one horizontal line at y = 600 px in every shot (the analogue of the Opus 5.5 arc).
Every shot puts the joint plane of its interconnect level on that line, in a different art style. The pitch is drawn
at a similar on-screen size in every shot, so the frame looks equally dense while the overlaid number falls by 3.4
decades.

Pitch ladder (overlay values; atlas `data/interconnects.csv` row counts at that exact pitch, 2026-10-22):

| Level | Interconnect | Pitch | Atlas rows |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | BGA (ball grid array) solder ball, package to PCB | 1 mm | 22 (`bga`) |
| L2 | C4 (controlled-collapse chip connection) solder bump | 150 um | 11 (`c4`) |
| L3 | Cu-pillar microbump with SnAg cap | 40 um | 24 (`microbump`) |
| L4 | Fine-pitch microbump in a TSV die stack (imec 3D stacking test vehicles) | 10 um | 8 (`microbump`) |
| L5 | Cu-Cu hybrid bond, die-to-wafer | 3 um | 6 (`cu_cu_hybrid`) |
| L6 | Cu-Cu hybrid bond, wafer-to-wafer | 1 um | 5 (`cu_cu_hybrid`) |
| L7 | Cu-Cu hybrid bond, wafer-to-wafer | 0.4 um | 5 (`cu_cu_hybrid`; e.g. ectc51909.2023.00008-a, iedm45741.2023.10413829-a) |

0.4 um rather than the atlas minimum 0.3 um (2 rows, design targets): more rows, more independent sources.

## Rules for scenes (full contract: `media/hybrid-bonding-short/STYLE.md`)

- Generated by code only; figure crops are visual references; no tracing, no embedded crop pixels, no copied layouts.
- Topology (stack order, materials, shapes) accurate; featured-interconnect dimensions to scale; sources recorded per
  scene in `scenes/<scene>.notes.md` (atlas row ids or citable public sources).
- Deterministic `render(t)`; no text inside scenes (the overlay is the only text); vendored OFL font.
